02

Giva Glow: a kitchen remedy, redesigned for modern skin

Ghee is trusted for dry skin but sticky and smelly. The brief: keep the heritage, remove the objections.

Academic project, University of the Punjab. Team of three. Concept with live Instagram and Facebook pages.

Problem

Dry skin, chemical creams, ghee people won't use raw.

Listening

Focus group: clean care, but the smell must go.

Position

Traditional remedy, modern convenience, affordable.

Form

Firm balm in a pocket tin. Proposed 1,000 PKR.

Story

Reels, micro-influencers, two TVC concepts.

Finding the open space

Natural but affordable was the open space. Tap a point.

High price Accessible price Natural Synthetic

Giva Glow: the natural composition of premium imports at a price close to mass products. The 'sweet spot' the team targeted.

Key decisions

Name: "Gi" for ghee, "Glow" for the result.

Scent: jasmine and rose tackle the biggest barrier.

Target: urban women, 18–35.

See the Instagram page See the Facebook page

Shows: turning an audience objection into naming, price and content.

03

Sugar Cane Bliss: a street favourite, made trustworthy

People love sugarcane juice but distrust roadside hygiene. Could a sealed, cold-pressed bottle fix that?

Academic concept, group of six. A proposal and pilot plan; no launch claimed.

The gap the team spotted

Roadside juice spoils fast and feels unhygienic, while busy buyers want sealed drinks.

"People love sugarcane juice, but they avoid it due to hygiene issues and short shelf life."

Positioning and product

Cold-pressed, sealed glass bottles in 250 ml and 500 ml, with lemon, mint and ginger.

Audience: health-conscious buyers, gym-goers, office workers.

From empathy to rollout

Design Thinking, step by step.

Shows: turning an everyday frustration into a tested business idea.
